# TCO, ROI & Business Case

Start with the current process baseline: volume, cycle time, labor, error/rework, infrastructure, revenue impact and risk.

**Annual net benefit = measurable business benefit − recurring AI operating cost**

**ROI = (benefit − total investment) / total investment**

Include inference/API, tokens/context, retrieval, storage, integration, compute, observability, security, evaluation, human review, engineering, change and support.

## Illustrative example
100,000 cases/year × €12 = €1.2M current annual process cost. A redesigned process at €8/case implies €400k gross potential benefit. If incremental AI/platform operations are €150k/year, simplified potential net operating benefit is €250k/year before implementation cost, adoption, risk adjustment and accounting effects.

Use sensitivity/scenario analysis instead of a single optimistic number.
