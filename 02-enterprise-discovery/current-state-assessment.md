# Current-State Assessment — BCPADI

| Lens | Discover | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Business | Outcomes, economics, constraints, KPIs | strategy, service and financial metrics |
| Capabilities | What the organization must be able to do | capability map, ownership |
| Processes | Work, decisions, approvals, exceptions | process maps, cycle/error rates |
| Applications | Systems of record, SaaS, custom/legacy | portfolio and dependencies |
| Data | Sources, ownership, quality, sensitivity | catalogs, schemas, documents, lineage |
| Integration | APIs, events, queues, files, devices | interface inventory, failure modes |

Also capture people, identity, infrastructure, regulation, operational risk and current unit economics.

For each major legacy component decide whether to **retain, wrap, integrate, replatform, refactor, replace or retire**.
