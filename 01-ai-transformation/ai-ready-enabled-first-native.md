# AI-Ready, AI-Enabled, AI-First and AI-Native

These terms describe different degrees of organizational and architectural dependence on AI.

| Level | Architecture signal | Question before advancing |
|---|---|---|
| AI-ready | governed data, usable interfaces, identity, controls and evaluation foundations | Can we introduce AI safely and measure it? |
| AI-enabled | AI augments selected capabilities | Is it improving a measurable outcome? |
| AI-first | AI is considered during capability/workflow redesign | Is AI the best default for this decision or step? |
| AI-native | AI is fundamental to the product/process | Does the added adaptivity justify dependence, cost and risk? |

Progression is **not a maturity race**. Different capabilities in the same enterprise can intentionally remain at different levels.
