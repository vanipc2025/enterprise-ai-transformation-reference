# Consultant Discovery Questions

**Business:** What outcome must change? Baseline? Cost of doing nothing?  
**Process:** Where are decisions, delays, exceptions and human judgment?  
**Legacy:** Systems of record? APIs? Technical debt? What must remain?  
**Data:** Ownership, quality, freshness, sensitivity and knowledge gaps?  
**Integration:** APIs, events, queues, files, devices; latency/availability?  
**AI:** Why AI? Simplest architecture? What happens when it is wrong?  
**Security:** What identities exist? What may an agent read/do? What needs approval/audit?  
**Operations:** How will quality, grounding, task success, latency, cost and incidents be measured?  
**Economics:** Current unit cost? Production unit cost? Adoption needed for ROI?  
**Migration:** Smallest safe transition state? Coexistence? Fallback/rollback?
